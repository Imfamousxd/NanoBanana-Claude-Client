# Rubric: realism-cinematic

Target: a photo that looks like it came off **real cinema or stills glass** — a commercial, an
editorial frame, a hero product shot. Deliberate light, deliberate composition.

Use `realism-ugc` instead for phone-shot/UGC/testimonial work. **Never apply both.** Shallow
depth-of-field and a controlled key are PASS criteria here and FAIL criteria there.

Deliberate is not the same as clean. The defect being hunted is the **rendered** look: light with
no source, highlights that never clip, glass with no aberration, surfaces with no wear. A real
camera is a physical instrument and leaves physical evidence. Judge only what you can actually
see; if a question cannot be resolved at this resolution, fail it and say so.

## BLOCK: LIGHT
- Can you identify a key light, and point at where it is coming from?
- Is there separation between subject and background — a rim, a kicker, or a tonal gap?
- Do shadows fall consistently with that single key, with no contradictory second shadow?
- Is there a real tonal range, with both genuine shadow and genuine highlight present?
- FAIL if the subject is lit from everywhere at once with no identifiable key.

## BLOCK: OPTICS
- Is there a clear focal plane, with a believable falloff into softness in front of and behind it?
- Is the falloff gradual and optical, rather than looking like a cut-out pasted onto a blurred plate?
- Is there any genuine lens evidence — slight vignetting, chromatic fringing, halation, or bokeh with shape?
- Is the image free of sharpening halos along high-contrast edges?
- FAIL if the background blur has a hard edge around the subject's outline.

## BLOCK: MATERIAL
- Do surfaces show physical evidence — grain, brushing, fingerprints, dust, wear, or micro-scratches?
- Do reflections on any glossy or metallic surface correspond to something plausibly in the room?
- Does the subject sit IN the scene, sharing its light and colour, rather than on top of it?
- Is there a contact shadow or reflection where the subject meets its surface?
- FAIL if every surface is flawless and reflection-free.

## BLOCK: COLOUR
- Does the grade have a direction while still holding a believable white point?
- Do highlights roll off smoothly rather than clipping to flat pure white?
- Are skin tones (if present) plausible rather than plastic or uniformly orange?
- FAIL if the entire frame is one uniform colour wash with no neutral anywhere.

## BLOCK: ARTEFACT
- Is the image free of unintended text, watermarks, captions and misspellings?
- Are hands, fingers and limbs anatomically correct and correctly counted?
- Is the image free of a device mockup, screen bezel, polaroid border or film-strip framing?
- Is any product label geometrically correct — text following the surface curve, not floating flat?
- Is the geometry coherent — no merged objects, no impossible joins, no duplicated features?
- FAIL on any of the above.

## BLOCK: COMPOSITION
- Is there ONE clear subject rather than several competing for attention?
- Is there deliberate negative space rather than every corner filled?
- Does any text or logo sit on a calm area rather than across busy detail?
- FAIL if the frame is cluttered enough that the eye has nowhere to rest.
