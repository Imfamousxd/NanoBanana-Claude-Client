## Why it's a useful template (maps to our laws)
- **Timestamped scene ladder** (Opening → 4 exercises → Closing), each a discrete beat with its own
  on-screen text — the `sd25:timed-beats-drive-multi-shot` + `sd25:30s story-map` pattern, executed.
- **Text overlays are the hook and the structure** — the opening "POV:" line is the scroll-stopper
  (`ugc:hook-rate-35pct`, `static-ad:hierarchy-is-the-hook`). NOTE: this is the ONE UGC format where
  burned-in text is correct — it's a faceless instructional listicle, not the talking-head lane where
  our `house` law bans on-screen text. Keep the two lanes separate.
- **One continuous subject, locked identity** (same man, beanie, headphones, mat, wall panels) across
  all beats — `sd25:continuity-locks-and-handoffs`.
- **Concrete, countable actions per beat** ("3x12", named exercise, named prop weight) — no vague
  "does a workout"; `sd25:detailed-prompt-formula` (one clear action per beat).
- **Soft motivational close** ("Repeat 3x a week & you'll get a 6 pack by summer") — payoff, not a
  hard "BUY NOW" (`ugc:soft-cta-beats-hard-cta`).

## The prompt (verbatim, for reuse — swap the subject/exercises/claims)
> Opening Scene (0:00 - 0:04): The video opens on a close-up shot of a shirtless man with a muscular
> physique, defined abs, wearing a black beanie and black over-ear headphones, kneeling on a grey
> workout mat in a gym setting. White text overlay across the screen reads: "POV: YOUR LOWER BELLY
> FAT IS GONE AFTER DOING THIS". He grabs his t-shirt with both hands, lifts it slightly to reveal
> his lower abdominals, and then uses his index fingers to point directly to them. He then places a
> white water bottle next to him on the floor, ready to begin the workout.
> Exercise 1: Seated Leg Raises (0:05 - 0:07): The text overlay changes to "Seated leg raises 3x12".
> The man, back to textured metal wall panels and fitness equipment (barbells and plates visible),
> transitions to a seated position, leans back supporting his weight on his hands behind him, and
> repeatedly raises both straight legs from just above the floor towards the ceiling, maintaining control.
> Exercise 2: In and Outs (0:08 - 0:10): Text overlay "In and outs 3x12". Seated, hands behind for
> balance, he brings both knees into his chest then extends them back out straight without touching the floor.
> Exercise 3: Cocoon Passes (0:11 - 0:13): Text overlay "Cocoon Passes 2x12". Lying flat on his back,
> he reaches overhead for a small black weighted plate (5-10 lbs), brings his feet up in a slight crunch,
> and passes the plate from above his head to his lower shins, engaging his core, then returns to start.
> Exercise 4: Russian Twist (0:14 - 0:16): Text overlay "Russian twist 3x12". Seated, leaning back,
> feet slightly elevated, he holds the weighted plate with both hands and twists his torso from side to
> side, touching the plate to the mat on each side.
> Closing Scene (0:17 - 0:20): Text overlay "Repeat 3x a week & You'll get a 6 pack by summer ☀". He
> sits, then kneels next to the water bottle, gesturing towards his abs again. Ends on an instructional,
> motivational note.
