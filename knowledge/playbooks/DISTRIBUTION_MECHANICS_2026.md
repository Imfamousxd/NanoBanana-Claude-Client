# Distribution mechanics — short-form recommendation systems, 2026

Shared by every NuLumin audience playbook (`AUDIENCE_*.md`). Segment playbooks supply audience
truth; this file supplies the physics. Neither overrides `knowledge/compliance` or the
regulated-content guardrails in `AGENTS.md`.

---

## 1. The thesis — design for the send, not the like

Both platforms rank on attention and forwarding, not approval.

- **Instagram:** watch time is the #1 ranking factor. Mosseri has confirmed watch time, **sends per
  reach**, and likes per reach as the three signals. Sends via DM are the strongest signal for
  reaching new audiences. Save rate, DM-share rate, and first-hour velocity carry the most weight;
  likes are close to a vanity metric. Reels are shown to **non-followers first** to gauge interest.
- **TikTok:** watch time and completion rate are 40–50% of the algorithm. Completion below ~70% is
  treated as weak; 60%+ on a 15-second video triggers a wider FYP push. Every video goes to a small
  test pool first — completion, rewatch, and early engagement velocity decide whether it escapes.

Every NuLumin priority segment sits inside a tight in-group with an active group chat — a crew, a
unit, a family thread, a sister-circle. **The content unit that wins is the one a peer sends to
another peer.** The creative target is not "will they like this" but **"will they send this to the
one person who will get it."**

Every brief must name the intended **sender → receiver** pair before anything else is written.

## 2. Structural formula

```
0.0–0.8s   Situation, not topic. A stated fact the in-group recognises instantly.
0.8–3.0s   Stakes or a number. This is the Instagram survival gate.
3–12s      One idea. One only. Deliver it with a visible object or document on screen.
12–18s     The turn — the thing that makes it worth forwarding.
Final beat No fade. Land on a punchline, a figure, or an open question.
```

Primary cut **12–22 seconds**. A 15-second video clearing 60% completion gets a wider FYP push;
under ~70% completion is treated as weak.

**Run two tiers from one shoot.** Reels up to three minutes now reach non-followers through
recommendations, and TikTok rewards niche authority over trend-chasing. The short cut wins
distribution; the long cut (60–180s COA walkthrough, vendor-vetting framework) converts and
accumulates search equity.

## 3. Social SEO — now equal to or larger than hashtags

Keyword-optimised captions, voiceovers, and on-screen text carry equal or greater weight than
hashtags for discovery. TikTok transcribes audio, so **spoken keywords rank**. Four placements,
every post:

1. **Spoken** — say the phrase aloud, naturally, in the first 10 seconds.
2. **On-screen text** — burned in; indexed and searchable.
3. **Caption** — natural language, intent-shaped. Instagram weights the first 125 characters.
4. **Hashtags** — niche only. Generic tags underperform specific ones.

**Cross-segment keyword core:** how to read a certificate of analysis · research peptide COA ·
what does research use only mean · HPLC purity explained · how to verify a peptide supplier ·
lot number verification · third-party tested peptides

Segment-specific keyword banks live in each `AUDIENCE_*.md`.

---

## 4. Cadence and testing

- **4–5 posts per creator per week.** Niche consistency beats volume; both algorithms reward topical
  authority around a repeated problem set.
- **The first hour is decisive.** Post inside the segment's real media window, not a marketing
  schedule. Windows are specified per segment.
- **Test the hook, not the video.** Re-cut the same body with three different first-two-seconds.
- **Kill fast.** Under 45% completion at 24 hours means the hook failed. Recut, don't boost.

## 5. Metrics, in priority order

| Rank | Metric | Why | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sends per reach** | Strongest new-audience signal; the in-group forward | >1.5% |
| 2 | **Completion rate** | 40–50% of TikTok ranking | >65% |
| 3 | **Saves per reach** | Purchase-intent proxy in this category | >2% |
| 4 | **3-second retention** | The Instagram survival gate | >80% |
| 5 | Comments | Sentiment only; watch for dosing questions | qualitative |
| — | Likes | Near-vanity in 2026 | do not optimise |

## 6. Creator brief template

```
SEGMENT:            (see AUDIENCE_*.md)
SENDER → RECEIVER:  who forwards this to whom, named specifically
SETTING:            exact place, time of day, what just happened
HOOK:               verbatim first line, ≤ 12 words
ONE IDEA:           the single thing this video says
PROOF OBJECT:       COA / lot number / box / vial — physically on screen
RUNTIME:            12–22s primary cut  (+ long cut if applicable)
SPOKEN KEYWORD:     exact phrase, said aloud in first 10s
ON-SCREEN TEXT:     burned-in, verbatim
CAPTION:            first 125 characters written out
DISCLOSURE:         #ad in first line + first 3 seconds — non-negotiable
RUO LINE:           legible in frame or pinned — non-negotiable
BANNED THIS BRIEF:  segment-specific never-say list
SKU + LINK:         hero SKU, affiliate code
```

## 7. Compliance overlay — inherits, does not replace

Full rules live in `knowledge/compliance` and the persona system. Short form for every brief:

**Never:** depict injecting, reconstituting, dosing, or ingesting · post before/after or outcome
testimonials · make disease claims (including soft forms like "fixed my back", "cured my insomnia")
· give dosing guidance · compare to a brand-name drug · let a licensed clinician say "I recommend."

**Always:** `For Research Use Only. Not for human consumption.` legible in the final asset ·
FTC disclosure in the first line and first three seconds · a pre-written redirect for
"how much do you take?" — *"Can't advise on that. I only cover sourcing and documentation."*

A disclaimer does not cure otherwise noncompliant creative. Automated review is advisory; a
qualified human owns the final call.
