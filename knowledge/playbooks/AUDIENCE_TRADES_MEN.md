## 2. The pain reality

- **86%** of construction workers report acute musculoskeletal pain in the past 12 months; **24%**
  report chronic pain.
- Musculoskeletal disorders account for **52%** of work-related ill health in construction.
- Construction fatal injury rate is **9.2 per 100,000** versus 3.3 across all industries.

Their body is not a hobby. It is the revenue-generating asset, uninsured against depreciation. A
shoulder that fails at 34 ends the earning curve at its peak. That is the emotional engine of this
segment, and it is not vanity.

## 3. Identity and language

**Identity line:** *"My body is the only tool I can't replace."*

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| beat up · wrecked · shot · nagging · flared up | fitness journey · wellness · self-care |
| my knees / my back / my shoulder | biohacking · optimisation · protocol |
| crew · foreman · shift · jobsite · rig | community · tribe · ritual |
| paperwork · lab sheet · third-party tested | curated · clinically formulated |

**Instant rejections:** gym-model physiques, aspirational lifestyle framing, wellness vocabulary,
condescension about their intelligence, anything that looks like an ad. This audience has a
finely-tuned detector for being sold to by someone who has never done the work.

**Credibility markers:** dirty hands, real tool wear, an actual jobsite, a truck cab, a lunch
cooler, work boots, a visible brace or tape job, correct trade jargon used casually.

## 4. Content formats

1. **Truck-cab monologue.** Parked, sound on, one take, 15–20s. Highest send-rate format — it reads
   as a text message, not a post.
2. **End-of-shift honesty.** Filmed dirty, 4 p.m., stating a physical fact plainly.
3. **The receipt.** Chiro visit, PT copay, lost day rate versus what documentation costs. Numbers on
   screen.
4. **The paperwork check.** Opening the box, pulling the COA, reading the lot number aloud. Zero
   claims, maximum trust, doubles as SEO because he speaks the keywords.
5. **Trade-specific injury taxonomy.** "Electricians get this. Plumbers get that. Welders get this."
   Very high forward rate — it is in-group taxonomy.
6. **The crew reaction.** Someone on site asks what it is; he answers in one sentence.

## 6. Visual and audio spec

Anchor to `Nulumin Generated/Themed Lifestyle/By Theme/Recovery/` and `.../Performance/` (12 shots
each). The locked NuLumin lifestyle look is **raw scanned 35mm, action-forward, never polished
HDR** — which happens to be exactly the register this audience trusts. Do not clean it up.

- Handheld, phone-native vertical. Auto-exposure recovery when he steps out of shade is a feature.
- Real ambient audio: diesel idle, compressor, radio bleed, wind. Room tone beats a clean mix.
- **Burn captions in.** Sound is off at work; assume the first view is silent.
- Product appears in a work context — truck console, toolbox lid, kitchen counter after a shift —
  never on a marble surface next to a plant.
