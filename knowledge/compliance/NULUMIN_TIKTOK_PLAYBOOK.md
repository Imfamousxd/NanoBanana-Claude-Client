# NuLumin — TikTok organic playbook (research-framed education)

Tailored operating guide for a NuLumin-branded TikTok presence, built on the July 2026 platform
research (`research/research-tiktok.md` + verification addendum) and NuLumin's existing compliance
OS (`../../NuLumin Influencer Personas/NULUMIN_INFLUENCER_PERSONA_SYSTEM.md` §3). Read
`PEPTIDE_MARKETING_COMPLIANCE.md` first. Not legal advice; the boundaries below are an enforcement
posture, not a grant of permission — a named human compliance reviewer owns final sign-off.

## The one thing to get right

There is **no official "research use only" lane** on TikTok — not in paid ads, not in TikTok Shop,
not in Branded Content. Any claim that RUO framing makes peptide marketing *permitted* is wrong at
the policy level. What is true, and what an experienced operator correctly observes surviving, is
narrower and specific:

> **Claim-free, commerce-free, non-weight-loss peptide *education* from a brand account persists on
> TikTok as a tolerated enforcement gap — reach-suppressed, age-gated, and revocable at any time.**

That is the entire footprint NuLumin gets. It is genuinely usable, but it is organic-only and it
lives on TikTok's discretion. Three hard perimeter facts:

1. **"Research use only" framing is disqualifying on TikTok Shop**, not protective. The chemicals
   policy bans products "intended for commercial, industrial, or professional use only," "not
   available for general consumer purchase," or "intended for use by medical professionals." A
   product NuLumin honestly describes as research-use fits that prohibited description by its own
   words. Plus "peptide hormones" are a named prohibited category and any **"GLP" in a product name
   or title is explicitly banned**. → **NuLumin does not sell on TikTok Shop.**
2. **Paid ads have no path** and a measured survival time of hours before takedown; a rejected/cloaked
   run risks the whole advertiser identity. → **NuLumin runs no TikTok ads for peptide SKUs.**
3. **The account link is the vulnerability, not the video.** TikTok's June 2025 bans used a "trade of
   regulated goods and services" theory: an account that routes viewers to a peptide storefront is
   "marketing/providing access" even when individual videos are claim-free. → **The TikTok identity
   must not link to, name, price, or code the store** (see §4).

The metabolic (GLP-1/2/3) and weight-loss angle is the highest-risk content on the platform and is
separately caught by TikTok's weight-management ban. **Keep NuLumin's TikTok identity anchored in
Cellular/Neural/Tissue-Repair education; keep Metabolic off TikTok almost entirely** (news
commentary at most, never product).

## 1. What this playbook is FOR

Top-of-funnel authority and audience-building for the biohacking/longevity/recovery viewer, feeding
the channels that actually convert (owned email/SEO, X, YouTube) per the segment-A strategy in
`PEPTIDE_MARKETING_COMPLIANCE.md` §4. TikTok is a **reach and credibility surface, not a storefront
and not a conversion surface.** Success = saves, follows, profile-visits-to-owned-destination, and
search presence for "what is <peptide>" — not TikTok-attributed sales.

## 3. NuLumin's five hard NOs → TikTok translation

NuLumin's existing contractual hard-NOs (persona doc §3.1) already cover the worst TikTok triggers;
TikTok adds two platform-specific ones:

- (Existing) No human-use depiction · no outcome testimonials/before-afters · no disease claims ·
  no dosing guidance · no brand-name drug comparisons. **All still apply, verbatim.**
- (TikTok-added) **No commerce signal of any kind from the TikTok identity** — no link, price, code,
  "shop," or DM-to-buy. The store and the TikTok account must be discoverable as the same brand only
  by a viewer choosing to search, never by TikTok being handed the path.
- (TikTok-added) **No product-as-merchandise display** (unboxing/vial-hero shots) — reserve for
  owned channels.

## 4. Profile & account hygiene

- **Bio:** brand name + a research-education positioning line + `For Research Use Only. Not for human
  consumption.` Do **not** put a store URL, "shop," "order," or a Linktree-to-checkout in bio. A link
  to a *non-commerce* education hub (glossary, COA-literacy resource) is the maximum defensible link,
  and even that is a judgment call the compliance reviewer signs off on.
- **On every video:** the RUO line legible in-frame or pinned (already a NuLumin mandatory element,
  persona §3.3).
- **Comments:** no dosing/sourcing coaching. Use NuLumin's pre-written redirect: *"Can't advise on
  that — these are research compounds; I only cover sourcing and documentation."* Pin it.
- **One identity, one job:** don't cross-post the store's product creative onto this account. The
  TikTok account's job is education; the store's job is sale. Keeping them functionally separate is
  the difference between "brand does education" and "account markets a regulated good."
- **Creators:** any cast creator inherits the full persona-doc compliance OS — pre-approved
  script/caption, unavoidable `#ad`/Paid-partnership disclosure in the first line and first 3
  seconds, no comment coaching, 90-day audit, indemnity clause. The agency is liable for their
  claims (FTC), so no creator ships unreviewed.

## 5. Weekly cadence (adapts NuLumin's rolling cadence to the matrix)

- **Week 1 — Mechanism explainer** (rotate Cellular → Neural → Tissue Repair; skip Metabolic).
- **Week 2 — Regulatory/category news commentary** (the reach driver; keep it non-promotional).
- **Week 3 — COA/testing literacy** (generic "how to read any COA," not our-lot-specific).
- **Week 4 — Vendor-vetting framework** (deliver the framework; stop before the pitch).

Expect suppressed/age-gated reach on most of this — that is the cost of the category and is
built-in, not a failure of the content.

## 6. Written acknowledgment for the client (put this in the SOW)

Before running a NuLumin TikTok account, the client should sign that they understand: this footprint
is a **discretionary enforcement gap, not TikTok permission**; content will be reach-suppressed;
accounts in this category are removed in waves following press/regulator attention (Oct 2024, Jun
2025, Feb 2026); TikTok is not a conversion channel and should carry no revenue target; and a single
commerce-signal slip (link/code/price) escalates from suppression to account ban. The agency builds
the defensible footprint; the client owns the residual platform risk.

## 7. Volatility

TikTok's direction of travel through 2025–2026 is *tightening*, not loosening (Jan 2025 supplement
docs regime, GLP naming ban, H1 2025 Shop purge of 70M listings / 700K sellers). The Feb–Apr 2026
FDA/RFK peptide *compounding* reclassification changed what licensed pharmacies may compound — it did
**not** change any TikTok policy and did nothing for RUO sellers (FDA escalated against them in
parallel). Do not build creative on "peptides are legal/allowed now." Re-verify this playbook before
each quarter.
