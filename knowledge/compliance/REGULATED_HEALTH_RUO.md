# Regulated health — Research Use Only profile

This profile is a conservative creative gate for research-use-only product marketing. It is not
legal or medical advice and cannot replace review by qualified counsel and the responsible brand
owner. Laws, enforcement posture, and platform policies change; every external rule needs a source
URL, jurisdiction/platform, retrieval date, and scheduled review date.

## Hard blocks

- Human consumption, injection, reconstitution, administration, dosing, titration, cycling, or a
  personal protocol.
- Before/after, personal outcome testimonial, transformation, or implied treatment result.
- Disease prevention, diagnosis, mitigation, treatment, cure, or drug-equivalence claim.
- Unsupported safety, efficacy, purity, origin, cGMP, third-party testing, certification, approval,
  or regulatory-status claim.
- Invented COA values, lot numbers, lab names, dates, signatures, seals, flags, or certification
  badges. A model-rendered document is decoration, not evidence.
- A disclosure used as permission for otherwise prohibited creative.

## Required controls

- The exact disclosure `For Research Use Only. Not for Human Consumption.` must be assigned to an
  approved placement (asset, overlay, caption, or platform field) and verified for final legibility.
- Any factual marketing claim must reference a current source record approved for that exact use.
- Paid/affiliate creator work requires an applicable, conspicuous sponsorship disclosure and human
  review of the post, caption, link, and comment-response plan.
- Final approval must record reviewer, date, territory/platform, and disposition. Automated scores
  inform the reviewer but do not approve publication.

## Safe creative territory

Subject to source verification: package craft, unboxing without human-use implication, document
navigation, lot/asset matching, neutral research-category education, sourcing questions, and
brand/process storytelling. A generated COA or label must never substitute for a real supplied
document.

## Source hygiene

The influencer strategy document uses `[S]` and `[M]` markers. Those markers are useful editorially
but are not machine-verifiable citations by themselves. Before a claim enters creative, store its
actual URL/document, excerpt or data field, owner, approval status, and expiry/review date in the
knowledge layer.
