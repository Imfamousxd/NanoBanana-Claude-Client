# Peptide marketing compliance — platforms + regulators (researched 2026-07-22)

Master synthesis of four sourced research reports in `research/` (Meta, TikTok, other platforms,
FDA/FTC). This document governs paid ads AND organic/creator content for every peptide-adjacent
brand in the catalog. It complements `REGULATED_HEALTH_RUO.md` (the creative gate) with the
platform-policy and enforcement layer. Not legal advice; policies drift — each claim's source URL
and retrieval date (2026-07-22) live in the `research/` files. Re-verify before relying on a
specific rule for a billable campaign.

## 1. The three brand segments (every rule depends on which one applies)

| Segment | Catalog examples | Regulatory identity |
|---|---|---|
| **A. RUO research peptides** | NuLumin, Noble Harbor, Aevum+ | Unapproved drugs the moment marketing implies human use. NOT supplements (drug-preclusion clause, FDCA §201(ff)(3)(B)) |
| **B. Clinical/telehealth GLP-1** | Dialed Health (Rx-adjacent SKUs) | Prescription-drug marketing; only lawful ad path is LegitScript certification + platform authorization |
| **C. True dietary supplements** | Dialed Moods, non-peptide wellness SKUs | Advertisable on most platforms with 18+ targeting, structure/function claims, substantiation |

## 2. The regulatory floor (applies to ALL content on every channel)

- **Intended-use doctrine (21 CFR 201.128):** a product's legal status is set by its total
  marketing context, not its label. An "RUO / not for human consumption" disclaimer is one data
  point FDA freely overrides. Things that convert an RUO vial into an unapproved new drug: any
  human-benefit claim (weight loss, appetite, healing, recovery, anti-aging), dosing/reconstitution
  content, human-result testimonials — **including liking or reposting someone else's** — lifestyle
  imagery implying self-administration, selling bacteriostatic water/syringes alongside vials,
  influencer posts describing personal use.
- **Enforcement is hot:** FDA warning letters to peptide vendors went 14 (2024) → 43+ YTD (2026);
  the 2026-03-31 wave hit seven RUO sites in one day and explicitly rejected the RUO disclaimer as
  a shield. DOJ criminal actions exist (Watkins indictment, Tailor Made $1.79M forfeiture). State
  AGs (CT, MS, multi-state) prosecute under UDAP statutes. Novo/Lilly sue and file NAD challenges.
- **FTC:** claims need competent & reliable scientific evidence (human RCTs) BEFORE publishing.
  ~670 companies hold Notice of Penalty Offenses (~$53k/violation exposure). Endorsement Guides
  (2023): disclosure must be **unavoidable** — spoken AND on-screen in video, persistent in lives,
  superimposed on Stories; platform "paid partnership" tools alone are insufficient; the brand AND
  the agency are liable for influencer claims. Fake Review Rule (16 CFR 465, eff. 2024-10-21):
  fake/AI/insider/incentivized-sentiment reviews and bought engagement = ~$51.7k per violation.
- **Supplement claims:** structure/function only ("supports healthy metabolism") + DSHEA
  disclaimer + 30-day FDA notification. Obesity is a disease → "weight loss" promises trend toward
  disease claims; "weight management" is materially safer. "Clinically proven/studied" is an
  establishment claim that invites class actions (Lemme GLP-1 Daily precedent) unless the finished
  product was tested. Never compare to Ozempic/Wegovy/Zepbound ("nature's Ozempic" = enforcement
  magnet). No "FDA approved" for anything compounded or RUO.
- **GLP-1 specifics:** shortage over (2025); mass compounding enforcement discretion ended
  2025-06-17; FDA hammers equivalence claims ("same active ingredient," "generic Ozempic").
  UK/EU: advertising prescription-only medicines to the public is illegal full stop (ASA runs
  monthly sweeps and now bans individual influencer discount-code posts).

## 3. Platform rules at a glance

### Paid ads

| Platform | Segment A (RUO) | Segment B (GLP-1 telehealth) | Segment C (supplements) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta** | ✗ no path; no "research chemical" category exists | ✓ via LegitScript (~$975 + $2,150/yr, 4–8 wks) + written Meta authorization; US/CA/NZ, 18+ | ✓ 18+, S/F claims; expect Jan-2025 data restrictions (no Purchase optimization) |
| **TikTok** | ✗ prohibited (unapproved drug / dangerous products); no research/RUO lane exists — ads survive hours. Organic education is a separate, tolerated gap (see below) | ✗ Rx ads prohibited in nearly all markets | ✓ with pre-approval docs (cGMP cert, label photos, lab report ≤180d), 18+; weight-loss supplements banned US/EU |
| **Google/YouTube** | ✗ (one narrow B2B lab-reagent lane w/ pharma-manufacturer cert, US/CA — genuine institutional suppliers only) | ✓ LegitScript + telemed cert BEFORE first ad | ✓ if no disease/Rx-equivalence claims |
| **X** | ✗ | ~ telemedicine w/ prior X authorization | ✗ supplements-with-claims prohibited (stricter than X's reputation) |
| **Reddit** | ✗ (steroids/HGH-adjacent banned) | ~ written pre-approval via sales rep | ✓ with restrictions (no Rx-equivalence) |
| **Pinterest** | ✗ | ~ Feb-2025 exception: Rx/compounded GLP-1 with clinical (not weight-loss) framing + approval | ~ weight-loss ban still total |
| **Snapchat** | ✗ | ~ pre-approval form | ~ no weight-loss supplements, no before/after |

### Commerce & branded content
- **Meta Commerce:** ingestible supplements AND Rx products are banned from Shops/product tagging
  entirely — even a fully compliant vitamin can't be tagged.
- **TikTok Shop:** "peptide hormones" are named prohibited products; **"GLP" in any product name or
  title is explicitly banned**; chemicals "for professional use only" or "not available for general
  consumer purchase" are prohibited — so **RUO framing disqualifies a listing rather than saving
  it**; supplements need invite-only category qualification (FDA facility registration, cGMP,
  accredited-lab COA ≤180 days, real label photos).
- **Branded content:** TikTok prohibits supplements/weight-loss/Rx as disclosed paid partnerships
  in nearly all markets (route creator work through Shop affiliate or Spark Ads instead); Meta
  requires the Paid Partnership label and bans branded content for drugs/unsafe supplements.

### Organic content (the "make no mistakes" rules)
What gets pages/accounts removed on every platform is the same triad: **product + price +
acquisition path** (link-in-bio to checkout, "DM to order," discount codes, affiliate codes) plus
**consumption instruction** (dosing, reconstitution, injection how-to). Neutral education mostly
survives but gets quietly reach-suppressed (TikTok age-gates regulated-substance discussion and
makes it For-You-ineligible; expect the same suppression dynamics elsewhere).

**On the "TikTok allows it for research purposes" belief (verified 2026-07-22).** Partly true, and
the true part is worth using — but it is not permission. There is **no official RUO/research lane**
in TikTok ads, Shop, or Branded Content; no policy text scopes the drug prohibitions to "human
consumption," so a research-labeled vial gets no literal exemption. On **Shop the RUO framing is
affirmatively disqualifying** — the chemicals policy bans products "intended for commercial,
industrial, or professional use only" or "not available for general consumer purchase," which is
what an honest RUO description says. Paid RUO ads have a documented survival time of hours. What
*does* persist is **claim-free, commerce-free, non-weight-loss peptide education from brand
accounts** — an enforcement gap plus a genuinely tolerated education niche, reach-suppressed and
revocable in waves whenever press or regulators look (Oct 2024, Jun 2025, Feb 2026). The account's
store link — not the individual video — is the ban vector, under TikTok's "trade of regulated goods"
theory. Build only inside that gap, with no commerce signal from the TikTok identity. NuLumin's
tailored version: `NULUMIN_TIKTOK_PLAYBOOK.md`. Note also that the Feb–Apr 2026 FDA/RFK peptide
*compounding* reclassification changed nothing on any platform and nothing for RUO sellers — it is
the most common source of "it's allowed now" confusion.

- Educational brand pages are permitted on Meta, TikTok, YouTube, X, Reddit.
- YouTube: education/mechanism content is fine and monetizable; vendor links or injection how-tos
  → removal/termination territory.
- X: densest biohacker audience, most permissive organic; watch Community Notes on efficacy claims.
- Reddit: biggest high-intent communities (r/Biohackers ~869K, GLP-1 subs ~200K each) but
  vendor/sourcing talk is removed by sub rules; brands limited to transparent AMAs + listening.
- Code words ("peppers," pepper emojis) are burned — journalists and moderators both know them;
  using them is evidence of evasion, not protection.
- FDA cites Instagram/Facebook/YouTube posts in warning letters — organic is discoverable evidence,
  not a safe harbor. Even liking a customer's testimonial is intended-use evidence.

## 4. Where peptide content performs (platform fit)

Audience center of gravity: men 25–45 biohacking/longevity/fitness + a female-skewing GLP-1
weight-loss cohort. Podcasts (Rogan/Huberman/Brecka ecosystem) and newsletters are where trust and
conversion actually happen; "peptide" surpassed "Ozempic" in US Google search interest (Apr 2026).

- **Segment A (RUO):** paid is closed everywhere. Realistic stack, in order: X organic →
  YouTube education → owned newsletter/email + SEO → Reddit listening/AMAs → (podcast reads =
  highest FTC risk). This confirms the existing NuLumin strategy: creator + SEO + email, "cast the
  verifier, not the testimonial" — creators sell sourcing literacy (COAs, testing, traceability),
  never outcomes.
- **Segment B (GLP-1 telehealth):** LegitScript is the single highest-leverage unlock (opens
  Google, YouTube, Meta, Pinterest, X-with-auth). Advertise the medical program/consultation, never
  the compounded drug or price-per-vial.
- **Segment C (supplements):** Google/YouTube + Meta paid, TikTok with pre-approval, creator
  content via Shop-affiliate/Spark rather than branded-content toggles.

## 5. Hard NOs for all catalog content (superset; see REGULATED_HEALTH_RUO.md)

1. No human-use depiction for RUO: no consumption, injection, reconstitution, dosing, protocol,
   or "cycle" content — and no bundling/depicting bac water or syringes with vials.
2. No outcome testimonials or before/after anywhere in weight-loss-adjacent work; no pinch-the-fat
   or body-inferiority creative; 18+ targeting always.
3. No disease claims; no drug-equivalence or drug-name comparisons; no "GLP" in product names or
   titles on TikTok surfaces; prefer internal aliases (GLP-1(S)/GLP-2(T)/GLP-3(R)) in briefs.
4. No commerce signals in organic for Segment A: no prices, codes, affiliate links, "DM to buy."
5. No undisclosed creator relationships: unavoidable, platform-native disclosure on every paid or
   gifted post; archive briefs and substantiation; never brief a creator with a claim the brand
   couldn't run as an ad.
6. No fake or incentivized-sentiment reviews, no bought engagement, no AI-generated testimonials.
7. No "FDA approved," "clinically proven," purity, or certification claims without a verified
   source record approved for that exact use.
8. No cloaked funnels, rented/agency ad accounts, or clean-domain/dirty-domain splits — they
   convert ad rejections into permanent business-level bans.
9. A disclaimer never licenses otherwise prohibited creative; regulators and platforms judge the
   total impression.
